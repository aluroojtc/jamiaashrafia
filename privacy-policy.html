Privacy Policy

Applies to the Jamia Ashrafia LMS mobile app (Android package com.jamiaashrafia.lms) and the LMS web portal at lms.jamiaashrafia.asia.
Effective date: 12 November 2026

In short
  • The app is the Learning Management System of Jamia Ashrafia Lahore. It is used by the institution's students, teachers and staff, and by guests invited to a meeting.
  • It handles the education records the institution keeps about you, and the audio, video and screen you choose to share in a live class or meeting.
  • It contains no advertising and no advertising or analytics trackers. We do not sell personal data.
  • Your data is held on servers operated for Jamia Ashrafia Lahore and is used only to run its teaching and administration.

1. Who is responsible

Jamia Ashrafia Lahore ("the institution", "we") operates the LMS and decides how the personal data in it is used. Contact details are at the end of this policy.

2. Information we collect

Account and profile. Accounts are created by the institution, not by signing up in the app. An account holds your name (in English and Urdu), your father's or guardian's name, roll number or staff ID, username, email address and phone number where given, gender, class, programme, campus, your role and the permissions that go with it, and the status of the account.

Sign-in details. Your password is stored on our server only as a salted, one-way hash; nobody at the institution can read it. When you sign in we record the time, your IP address and the type of device or browser, and we keep a log of sign-ins, failed sign-in attempts and password changes.

Education records. Class and course enrolment, timetable, attendance (including the time you join an online class), assignments and the work and files you submit, exams, answers to online papers, marks, results and teachers' remarks, library loans, and notices sent to you.

Admission records. Details given on an admission application, which may include a CNIC / B-Form or passport number, country, previous school and contact details. Identity document numbers are visible only to staff who are specifically authorised to see them.

Fees. Fee challans, amounts, payment status, the bank reference you give and any payment proof you upload. The app does not take payments and does not collect card or bank account details.

Live classes and meetings. When you join a live class or meeting, your display name, your audio, your camera video and your screen are transmitted only while you switch them on. Chat messages, raised hands and reactions are sent to the other participants. The app joins with the microphone and camera switched off unless you are the host.

Recordings. A class or meeting may be recorded by its host or by the institution. A recording contains the audio, video, shared screens and names of the people who took part. Hosts may also allow a participant to record a meeting on that participant's own computer.

Guests of a public meeting. A person who joins a meeting through a public link, without an LMS account, is asked only for a name to show in the meeting. We also receive the IP address of the connection.

Files you upload. Documents, images and other files you attach to assignments, payment proofs or course material.

Activity log. For security and accountability we log important actions such as changes to accounts, roles, results, payments and meeting settings, with the account that made them.

The app does not collect your location, contacts, calendar, photos or messages, and does not read data from other apps.

3. Permissions the mobile app uses

  • Camera and microphone: only inside a live class or meeting, when you switch them on.
  • Screen capture: only when you start sharing your screen in a meeting. Android asks you to confirm each time, and sharing stops when you stop it or leave the meeting.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show that a meeting or screen share is in progress and keep it running while the app is in the background.
  • Bluetooth and audio settings: to use a headset or speaker during a meeting.
  • Internet access: to reach the LMS and the meeting server.

You can withdraw the camera, microphone and notification permissions at any time in your device's settings. The rest of the app keeps working; you will not be able to speak or show video in a meeting until they are allowed again.

4. What is stored on your device

The mobile app stores a session token in the device's secure storage so you stay signed in. It does not store your password. Your records are kept in memory only while the app is open and are cleared when you sign out. The web portal uses one sign-in cookie and stores your language choice and a working copy of the records you are allowed to see in your browser; these are cleared when you sign out.

5. How we use the information

  • To identify you and give you access only to what your role allows.
  • To run teaching and administration: classes, timetables, attendance, assignments, exams, results, fees, the library and notices.
  • To hold live classes and meetings and, where a class is recorded, to let those who are entitled watch it later.
  • To keep the system secure, investigate misuse and keep a record of who changed what.
  • To meet the institution's obligations to keep academic and financial records.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not make decisions about you by automated means alone.

6. Who can see your information

  • Within the institution. Access follows roles and campuses. A student sees their own records and their class's material. A teacher sees the classes they teach. Office staff and administrators see what their duties require.
  • Other participants in a meeting see your name and whatever audio, video, screen and chat you share. People who can open a recording see the same.
  • Service providers. The LMS and its meeting server (meet.jamiaashrafia.asia) run on servers rented by the institution from a hosting provider, which stores the data on our behalf and does not use it for its own purposes. If the institution sets a class to be held on an outside service such as Zoom, Google Meet or Microsoft Teams, joining that class takes you to that service, whose own privacy policy then applies.
  • Web portal resources. Pages of the web portal load fonts and icons from Google Fonts and cdnjs (Cloudflare). As with any website, those providers receive your IP address when your browser fetches these files.
  • Legal requirements. We may disclose information where the law or a competent authority requires it, or to an examination board the institution is affiliated with where that is part of your studies.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

7. How long we keep it

  • Sign-in sessions expire automatically after 12 hours and are removed when you sign out or change your password.
  • Recordings are deleted automatically after the retention period set by the administration, unless a recording is deliberately kept for teaching.
  • Guest details for a public meeting are not kept as an account; the meeting link stops working when the meeting ends.
  • Academic, admission and fee records are kept for as long as you are enrolled or employed and afterwards for as long as the institution must keep them as educational and financial records.
  • Security and activity logs are kept for as long as needed to investigate problems and show who made a change.

8. How we protect it

  • All traffic between the app or your browser and our servers is encrypted in transit (HTTPS / TLS). Meeting audio and video are encrypted in transit to the meeting server.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes, repeated wrong passwords temporarily lock sign-in, and temporary passwords must be changed at first use.
  • Every request is checked on the server against your role, permissions and campus. Live classes admit only people the server has authorised.
  • Uploaded files and recordings are served only to signed-in users who are allowed to see them.

No system can be guaranteed completely secure. If you believe your account has been used by someone else, change your password and tell the Academic Office at once.

9. Children and students under 18

Some students are under 18. Their accounts are created by the institution as part of their enrolment, with the involvement of a parent or guardian in the admission process, and are used only for their education. The app is not intended for the general public or for children who are not enrolled, and nobody can create an account in it by themselves.

10. Your choices and rights

  • You can see most of your own records in the app and the portal, and change your password and language there.
  • You can ask the institution for a copy of your personal data, to correct something that is wrong, or to delete your account and data. Where the institution is required to keep a record (for example examination results or fee payments), it will tell you what must be kept and why.
  • You can keep your camera and microphone off in a meeting, and you will be told when a meeting is being recorded.

To make a request, contact the Academic Office using the details below. We may need to confirm your identity first.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how personal data is handled, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be announced through the LMS.
